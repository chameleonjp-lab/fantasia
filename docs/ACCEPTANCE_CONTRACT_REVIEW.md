# Acceptance contract review

Reviewed baseline: PR #6 head `9ba68ce40fa8db40fbe17df34d638cffa0cdb6dc`, 2026-10-06 UTC. This clarification does not revise product thresholds, approve publication, or replace the complete F-01–F-20 evidence matrix.

## Keep these categories separate

1. **Explicit product rule: 250 ms / 15 unprocessed ticks.** [Specification §8.1](FANTASIA_SPEC.md#81-時計) requires a recorded performance stop when the unprocessed accumulator exceeds 0.25 seconds, with no catch-up across the pause boundary and no ordinary fastest-record entry for that run. `src/main.ts` implements the check as `accumulator + dt > .25` while running. This is not an arbitrary assertion timeout. A guard firing as specified is safety evidence, while the interrupted run still fails the uninterrupted acceptance observation.
2. **Provisional protection policy: 1000 ms fence guard.** `src/render-queue.ts` sets `STALL_AFTER_MS = 1000` for CPU-observed outstanding-frame time. No named-device measurement or product-spec derivation for that exact threshold was found in the reviewed sources and evidence. It should remain a separately identified protection policy pending calibration; that finding does not justify silently raising it, bypassing it, or treating all safety stops as environmental.
3. **Test orchestration bounds.** `playwright.config.ts` sets 60 s per test and 15 s per assertion; `tapLiveControl` in `browser-tests/fantasia.spec.ts` uses 2.5 s per tap. These limit test operations; they are not frame-time, touch-response or named-device performance targets. The 15 s renderer preparation watchdog in `src/main.ts` is a separate startup protection bound, despite sharing a numerical value with the assertion timeout.
4. **Initial preparation versus steady live frame.** Specification §8.1 requires ready-before-start and freezes logical time during loading/home. Baseline `CampaignScene.prepare()` compiles, primes selected effects and waits for initial submitted work; `main.ts` enables Start only after it resolves. `begin()` subsequently creates a fresh campaign and submits a gameplay frame. Preparation completion alone does not prove every first-live-frame resource/path is ready. Legitimate preparation must complete before input/live time begins; startup work must not be hidden by discarding live ticks or relaxing the running safety rules. Startup and steady-state observations should be labeled separately.
5. **Functional completeness versus physical performance.** [Specification §9.2](FANTASIA_SPEC.md#92-性能予算未測定の受入目標) and [implementation plan P7](FANTASIA_IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md#p7-回帰公平性性能バランス) require a named desktop and phone, 470 actors with maximum-lane firing for at least 120 s, frame and per-tick distributions, a 20-minute run and 10 restarts with leak checks. Desktop targets are 1440×900/DPR1, 60 fps, p95 frame ≤20 ms and p95 simulation tick ≤6 ms. Phone targets are 393×852/852×393, DPR3/internal cap 1.5, ≥30 fps, p95 frame ≤33.3 ms and p95 tick ≤10 ms. Record device, OS/browser/version, power settings, rendered DPR, logical/shot counts and workload. Named-device evidence is absent from the supplied CI evidence. The current Chromium runner explicitly selects SwiftShader and is not proven to be either specified physical target. Viewport/DPR emulation and pure simulation timing cannot fill that gap.
6. **False acceptance holes.** Earlier geometry success followed by a paused final snapshot cannot certify usable live flight; retain final-state, overlay and logic-safety checks. Conversely, a later pause must not relabel an earlier genuine overlap/layout assertion. Baseline input audit stores only input changes, so one `bomb:true`/`loop:true` entry is not proof that exactly one tick consumed the action. Require a lossless tick range or consecutive false boundary, zero dropped evidence and the expected gameplay effect. Missing, malformed or truncated evidence is explicitly incomplete.

CPU-observed fence completion latency (`lastCompletedPendingMs`) includes the elapsed interval from submission until the CPU observes completion. A GPU timer-query sum measures the query's GPU execution intervals. They are different metrics; neither should be renamed to the other or substituted for the specified frame/tick distributions. No GPU timer sum or named-device GPU pass is established by this report.

## Evidence and CI interpretation

Run `node scripts/report-browser-acceptance.mjs` after Playwright. Defaults are `test-results/browser-results.json`, named captures in `test-results/evidence`, and JSON/Markdown summaries in `test-results`. Optional `--results`, `--evidence` and `--out` paths support downloaded artifacts.

- Raw Playwright statuses, attempts, errors, skip annotations, attachments and statistics are retained. No failed check becomes passed. A flaky retry retains its failed attempt and cannot become a clean report pass.
- The annotation “functional observation blocked after recorded safety stop” requires one recognized live-phase/overlay/final-safety failure in `expectHudSafeLayout`, its matching highlighted failure location, a known title/artifact mapping and complete ordered capture observations establishing the stop. Earlier functional assertion errors, extra errors, unsupported helpers, ambiguous mappings and missing captures remain unclassified failures.
- First-touch bomb/loop proof references come from Playwright's actual `first-touch-*-input-audit.json` attachment metadata, including nested artifact paths or inline bodies. Their declared validation is recorded, not independently certified by this reporter. Absent attachments are reported; a successful isolated pulse is still only part of F-03.
- Missing browser output is `not-run`; malformed output/evidence is explicit. A valid report-writing process may exit zero even when it records browser failures: this only means the report was emitted. Missing/malformed browser input exits 2. The existing browser step remains the source of its own exit status.
- The proposed `verify.yml` change only adds an `if: always()` reporter step after browser execution and before the existing artifact upload. Every existing command, trigger, permission and failure path remains. No `continue-on-error`, required-check change or branch-protection change is proposed.

Correct safety behavior is not completed F-03/F-07 acceptance. Unit/build success plus annotations cannot produce comprehensive green acceptance. There is no conclusion that all gates are wrong, or that all software-renderer failures are environmental.
